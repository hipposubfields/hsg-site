Working Groups

The working groups of HSG

The HSG consists of multiple collaborative working groups dedicated to developing a comprehensive segmentation protocol for the hippocampus and MTL cortex. As a reference point for our proposed rules, we use histological annotations by leading experts on MTL anatomy. The proposed rules also undergo a collective evaluation by the broader community, enabling feedback and iterative refinement, if necessary. Our objective throughout this process is to foster effective communication and active engagement with the wider community. Additionally, we strive to facilitate accessibility to valuable resources that can aid individuals interested in MTL segmentation.

Active working groups

Histology Working Group

Aims to provide anatomical validity for the harmonized protocol through providing histological reference materials to our Boundary Working Groups.

Boundary Working Groups

Aims to develop a protocol for establishing subfield boundaries based on known anatomy. The group consists of several active smaller working groups targeting specific issues: a Head Group (inner and outer boundaries of the hippocampal head), a Tail Group (outer boundaries of the hippocampal tail), and an MTL cortex group (entorhinal, BA35 & BA36, and parahippocampal cortex).

Questionnaire Working Group

Aims to develop and compile data that seeks to better understand current practices in HS segmentation and to assess agreement on the developed segmentation rules.

Communication Working Group

Aims to actively work with reach-out about the efforts of the hippocampal subfields group, bringing both news-related content and pedagogical content for members of the community.

Completed working groups

Acquisition Working Group

Developed and established recommendations for MRI acquisition protocols that are optimal for hippocampal subfield segmentation.

Boundary Working Group: Hippocampal body

Recently finished the final assessments of the harmonized protocol for the hippocampal body.

Recruitment

Currently there are no working groups actively recruiting. However, if you are interested in participating in our efforts at some point, feel free to reach out and we will consider you for future recruitments — things we’d like to know about include your current research interests, experience with the HSG community, what you could add as part of a potential working group, and what sort of working group interests you.
