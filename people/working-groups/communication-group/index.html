Communication Group

General aims

Throughout the process of developing our harmonized protocol, it is important to foster effective communication and active engagement with the wider community. We are greatly appreciative of the ongoing interaction with a large community that allows us to receive feedback throughout the steps of the way!

Additionally, we strive to facilitate accessibility to valuable resources that can aid individuals interested in MTL segmentation. We try to maintain our various news outlets up to date with news of our efforts as well as resources as they become available.

Check out the HSG YouTube channel where resources related to hippocampal subfield segmentation are already available, and more to come!

We also host a podcast covering various topics related to the effort. Stay tuned!
