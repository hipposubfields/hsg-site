Questionnaire Working Group

General Aims

The aim of this working group is to develop and analyze data from a series of online questionnaires that seek to:

  • Characterize the following about HSG members: (1) years of experience with hippocampal subfield segmentation, (2) the field strength (3T/7T), image type (T1/T2, structural/functional), and image resolution used when segmenting the hippocampus, and (3) the population studied (e.g., younger/older/clinical populations)
  • Assess agreement of the broader HSG community with segmentation rules developed by the Boundary Working Group such that consensus can be reached using a Delphi approach
