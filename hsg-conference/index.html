HSG Conferences

The Hippocampal Subfields Group holds conferences one or more times a year. This includes both smaller working group meetings for developing aspects of the harmonized protocol, as well as larger meetings designed to disseminate and discuss progress made by these working groups. HSG meetings are open to HSG members and non-members alike.

Past Meetings

Hippocampal Subfields Group Meeting 19: Bordeaux, France – 2026

19–20 June · University of Bordeaux, Talence Campus

Following the annual conference of the Organization for Human Brain Mapping, the HSG hosted a symposium and a segmentation workshop. The symposium had 30 attendees and included presentations spanning a variety of topics related to hippocampal structure and function. The workshop had 24 attendees and included the Harmonized Body Protocol and the recently finalized Harmonized Tail Protocol. Updates were also provided on the Hippocampal Head Working Group. Special thanks to Dr. Robin de Flores for organizing the workshop; Dr. Ana Daugherty, Dr. Rosanna Olsen, and Gustaf Rådman for leading it; Dr. Marshall Dalton for updates on the Head Working Group; Savannah Tremblay for in-person training support; and Léa Chauveau for on-site organizational support.

Hippocampal Subfields Group Meeting 18: Detroit MI, USA – 2026

7–8 May · Wayne State University

The HSG hosted an in-person training workshop on the Harmonized Body Protocol for hippocampal subfield segmentation. There were 18 attendees with a variety of experience or knowledge in MRI, medial temporal lobe anatomy, and hippocampal segmentation. Special thanks to Drs. Ana Daugherty and Roya Homayouni for leading the workshop!

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 17: Toronto, Canada – 2024

11–12 April · Baycrest Academy for Research and Education

On the days prior to Cognitive Neuroscience Society's yearly conference, the HSG hosted an in-person workshop aimed at teaching the now final harmonized body protocol for hippocampal subfields. In total we had 30 attendees. Thanks to Ana Daugherty and Rosanna Olsen for leading the workshop; Thackery Brown, Kelsey Canada and Gustaf Rådman for training support; and Negar Mazloum-Farzaghi and Hannah Tucker for creating workshop resources.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 16: Baltimore MD, USA – 2023

9–10 November · Johns Hopkins University, Baltimore MD, USA

In the lead-up to SfN, the hippocampal head working group convened over two days to advance the development of the hippocampal head segmentation protocol. In addition to official working group members Marshall Dalton and Renaud La Joie, the meeting welcomed Kim Nguyen, Ana Daugherty, Kelsey Canada, Carl Hodgetts, and Rosanna Olsen. Thanks to Arnold Bakker for hosting.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 15: Lund, Sweden – 2023

16–18 October · Lund University, Lund, Sweden

The MTL working group gathered in Lund to further their efforts in developing a unified MTL protocol. Additionally, a Marcus Wallenberg Symposium in the form of an online webinar was conducted to delve into recent advancements made by the HSG working groups.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 14: Albacete, Spain – 2023

6–9 June, 2023 · University of Castilla-La Mancha, Albacete, Spain

A two-day neuroanatomy workshop, including a hands-on brain dissection session, and a two-day working group meeting continuing efforts for both the hippocampal head and MTL cortex. Organizers: Valerie Carr, Gustaf Rådman, Jenna Adams, Marshall Dalton, Laura Wisse, Ricardo Insausti, Mónica Muñoz López, and the Human Neuroanatomy Laboratory at University of Castilla-La Mancha.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 13: San Diego CA, USA – 2022

10–11 November, 2022 · UCSD Campus (Medical School Campus)

This meeting kicked off the MTL Cortex Working Group, beginning development of the MRI protocol for segmenting the entorhinal, perirhinal, and parahippocampal cortices. Organizers: Rosanna Olsen, Gustaf Nilsson, Jenna Adams, Laura Wisse, Christine Smith.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 12: Lund, Sweden – 2022

9–10 June, 2022 · Biomedical Center at Lund University

Aimed to characterize discrepancies in labelling parahippocampal subregions in ex vivo histological material, contributing toward a harmonized segmentation protocol on in vivo MRI (HS3.13). Organizers: Hannah Baumeister, Anika Wuestefeld, David Berron, Laura Wisse. Funding: Markus Wallenberg Stiftelse & Wenner-Gren Stiftelserna.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 11: San Diego CA, USA – 2018

3 November, 2018 · The Keating Hotel, San Diego, CA

Provided HSG members and the broader community with updates regarding the progress of the Questionnaire Working Group and Boundary Working Group. Many thanks to Valerie Carr for organizing.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 10: Magdeburg, Germany – 2018

18–19 October, 2018 · German Centre for Neurodegenerative Diseases (DZNE)

A small meeting of the Boundary Working Group finalizing a draft segmentation protocol for the hippocampal head and beginning work on the hippocampal tail. Generously funded by the DZNE and Emrah Duzel. Thanks to David Berron, John Shine, and Laura Wisse for organizing.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 9: Irvine CA, USA – 2018

16–17 April, 2018 · UC Irvine

A small meeting of the Boundary Working Group continuing efforts from London, focused on harmonizing segmentation rules for the hippocampal head. Thanks to Valerie Carr, Rosanna Olsen, and Manuella Yassa for organizing.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 8: Washington D.C., USA – 2017

11 November, 2017 · Washington, DC

Prior to the annual meeting of the Society for Neuroscience, the group met to provide an update on harmonization efforts, encourage participation in the first round of the questionnaire process, and share research findings. Thanks to Arnold Bakker for organizing.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 7: London, England – 2017

14 July, 2017 · London, England

A smaller meeting of the Boundary Working Group continuing efforts to develop an initial protocol for segmenting the hippocampal head. Made possible by the JPND 2016 call for “Working Groups for Harmonisation and Alignment in Brain Imaging Methods for Neurodegeneration.” Thanks to Martina Bocchetta for hosting.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 6: Montreal, Canada – 2017

2–3 April, 2017 · Douglas Mental Health University Institute, Montreal

A smaller meeting of the Boundary Working Group providing an update on the harmonization effort for the hippocampal body and working toward an initial protocol for the hippocampal head. Thanks to Mallar Chakravarty for hosting.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 5: San Diego CA, USA – 2016

11 November, 2016 · San Diego, CA

A two-part meeting: a smaller Boundary Working Group session at UCSD, followed by a day open to the broader HSG and interested SfN attendees, including an afternoon research symposium. Thanks to Christine Smith, Rosanna Olsen, and Valerie Carr for hosting.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 4: Chicago IL, USA – 2015

15–16 October, 2015 · Northwestern University, Chicago

A two-day retreat where the group developed a first draft of a harmonized protocol for subfield segmentation of the hippocampal body, working through boundaries for CA1-Subiculum, CA1-2-3, and CA3-DG. Thanks to Lei Wang for hosting.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 3: Irvine CA, USA – 2014

17–18 August, 2014 · University of California, Irvine

Hosted by Mike Yassa and the Center for Neurobiology of Learning and Memory. The group laid out a procedure for future progress and made headway on a heuristic-based boundary proposal for CA1-subiculum.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 2: San Diego CA, USA – 2013

9 November, 2013 · Satellite meeting, Society for Neuroscience

Discussed quantitative analyses of segmentation submissions, identified subfields with the most disagreement among segmenters, and worked as a group toward a unified segmentation.

Hippocampal Subfields Group meeting 1: Davis CA, USA – 2013

21–22 June, 2013 · University of California, Davis

The founding summit of the group. Special thanks to Cam Carter, Director of the Center for Neuroscience at UC Davis, who funded the catering.
