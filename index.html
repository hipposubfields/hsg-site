A worldwide neuroimaging consortium

Bringing consensus to how the hippocampus is mapped.

Hippocampal subfields are complex in both structure and function. For over a decade, the HSG has worked with the neuroimaging community toward a harmonized protocol for segmenting them on MRI — now extending to the surrounding MTL cortex.
