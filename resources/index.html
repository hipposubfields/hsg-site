Resources and recommendations

Protocols and Sample Data

We provide a single subject’s MRI dataset at reasonably high resolution to provide a common dataset on which to compare different protocols for subfield segmentation. Labeled datasets were formatted using a common scheme and served as the basis for comparison and discussion at HSG meetings.

See the documentation for information about the imaging data included in the evaluation dataset, segmentation formatting requirements, and deadlines. The full dataset can be downloaded from NITRC (registration required).

Please email Paul Yushkevich with questions regarding the quantitative component.

Acquisition recommendations

Early in our efforts we established guidelines for the type of MRI scans most useful for delineating hippocampal subfields, including recommendations on the degree of segmentation reasonable given a set of image data, and links to detailed descriptions of manual and automatic segmentation protocols. See the Acquisition Working Group page.

Publications and conference presentations

Publications and White Papers

At the August 2014 HSG meeting in Irvine CA, we put forward a vision for developing a harmonized segmentation protocol — see the white paper. It was followed by several collaborative publications:

  1. Yushkevich, P.A., et al. for the Hippocampal Subfield Group (HSG) (2015). Quantitative comparison of 21 protocols for labeling hippocampal subfields and parahippocampal cortical subregions in in vivo MRI: initial steps towards a harmonized segmentation protocol. Neuroimage 111:526-41.
  2. Wisse, L.E.M., Daugherty A., Olsen, R.K., et al. for the Hippocampal Subfield Group (HSG) (2017). A harmonized segmentation protocol for hippocampal and parahippocampal subregions: why do we need one and what are the key goals? Hippocampus. 27(1)3-11.
  3. Olsen, R. K., Carr, V. A., Daugherty, A. M., la Joie, R., … Wisse, L. E. M. (2019). Progress Update from the Hippocampal Subfields Group. Alzheimer's & Dementia: Diagnosis, Assessment & Disease Monitoring, 11, 439-449.
  4. Wuestefeld, A.*, Baumeister, H.*, et al. (2024). Comparison of histological delineations of medial temporal lobe cortices by four independent neuroanatomy laboratories. Hippocampus, 1–20.
  5. Canada, K.L.*, Mazloum-Farzaghi, N.*, Rådman, G., et al. (2024) A (Sub)field Guide to Quality Control in Hippocampal Subfield Segmentation on High-resolution T2-weighted MRI. Human Brain Mapping, 45(15), e70004.
  6. Daugherty, A.M.*, Carr, V.*, Canada, K.L., Rådman, G., et al., Hippocampal Subfields Group. (2026). Harmonized Protocol for Subfield Segmentation in the Hippocampal Body on High-Resolution In Vivo MRI From the Hippocampal Subfields Group (HSG). Hippocampus, 36(2), e70073.
