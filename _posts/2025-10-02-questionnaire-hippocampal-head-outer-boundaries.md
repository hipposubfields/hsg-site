  <p>We are seeking your response to a questionnaire relating to the Hippocampal Subfield Group's (HSG) segmentation protocol for the hippocampal head.</p>
  <p>A bit of background: the HSG aims to develop a harmonized protocol for segmenting human hippocampal subfields on MRI. We have already finalized the protocols for segmenting the hippocampal body (preprint <a href="https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.29.651039v1.">here</a>) and tail (manuscript in preparation). The current questionnaire pertains to the outer boundaries of the hippocampal head.</p>
  <p>Responses to the questionnaire are requested by <strong>Friday, October 17, 2025</strong>. We anticipate it will take approximately 45-60 minutes to complete, plus additional time as desired to review supplemental materials. We encourage you to review the questionnaire as a group, and ask that each group submit only a single response.</p>
  <p><strong>If you did not receive the email</strong> containing the survey link and PDF, please contact Dr. Valerie Carr (<a href="mailto:valerie.carr@sjsu.edu">valerie.carr@sjsu.edu</a>, Questionnaire Working Group Chair). To receive future mailings, join our <a href="https://groups.google.com/g/subfield_harmonization">Google Group</a>.</p>
  <p>We are happy to answer any questions &mdash; please contact Dr. Carr with questions related to this protocol, or <a href="mailto:hippocampalsubfieldsgroup@gmail.com">hippocampalsubfieldsgroup@gmail.com</a> for general questions. Many thanks for providing this valuable feedback.</p>
  <p>Kind regards,<br>Kelsey Canada on behalf of the HSG</p>
