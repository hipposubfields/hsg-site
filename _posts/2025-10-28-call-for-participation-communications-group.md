  <p>Dear HSG Community,</p>
  <p>In the coming months, we aim to expand the <a href="{{ '/people/working-groups/communication-group/' | relative_url }}">Communications Working Group</a> by adding a new Communications Chair and one general member. The purpose of the Communications Working Group is to facilitate communication with both the HSG and broader scientific communities &mdash; providing updates on our progress, information about upcoming events, opportunities for involvement, and other news.</p>
  <p><strong>Role of Communications Working Group Members</strong><br>
  Members support these efforts by writing and disseminating educational information about hippocampal subfields and the MTL on our social media platforms (X, Bluesky, YouTube), other outlets (podcast/interview videos, yearly newsletters), and our website. Members are also welcome to suggest additional ways to connect with the HSG community.</p>
  <p>Participation is ideal for those interested in developing science communication skills. If interested, email <a href="mailto:hippocampalsubfieldsgroup@gmail.com">hippocampalsubfieldsgroup@gmail.com</a> by <strong>December 1st</strong>.</p>

  <h3>Become Our New Communications Chair</h3>
  <p>The Communications Chair provides leadership and strategic direction for HSG's communication efforts:</p>
  <ol>
    <li><strong>Website Oversight</strong> &mdash; overseeing maintenance of the HSG website</li>
    <li><strong>Coordination of Communications Platforms</strong> &mdash; overseeing communication across multiple media platforms</li>
    <li><strong>Newsletters</strong> &mdash; compiling annual summary updates about group progress and forthcoming opportunities</li>
    <li><strong>Managing HSG membership</strong> &mdash; responding to member emails and distributing listserv updates</li>
    <li><strong>Outreach and Growth</strong> &mdash; developing new opportunities and channels for outreach</li>
  </ol>
  <p>The Communications Chair is a de facto member of the HSG Executive Steering Committee, which meets virtually for one hour each month. Overall time commitment varies through the year, averaging approximately <strong>8 hours per month</strong>.</p>
  <p><strong>Desired Qualifications:</strong> organized and detail-oriented; experienced in science communication; familiarity with WordPress or other website tools; extensive experience with Bluesky, X, YouTube and/or other social platforms; postdoc level or beyond preferred.</p>

  <h3>Become a General Member</h3>
  <p>Estimated time commitment is <strong>2 hours per month</strong>, with flexible tasks as listed above. Individuals from all career levels are welcome to apply.</p>

  <h3>How to Apply</h3>
  <p>Send the following to <a href="mailto:hippocampalsubfieldsgroup@gmail.com">hippocampalsubfieldsgroup@gmail.com</a>: first and last name, email address, current position, affiliation, a short statement of relevant experience (150 words max), and a short CV (5 pages max).</p>
  <p><em>Application deadline: December 1st</em></p>
  <p>Thank you very much for your interest in supporting the HSG Communications Working Group!</p>
